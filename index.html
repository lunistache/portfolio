Welcome to my portfolio. It's a small solar system: each planet is a part of my work, and the moons around it are the things inside.

  • Click a planet to fly to it and read about it.
  • Click a moon, then click it again to open it: my CV, or a live website.
  • Drag to look around and scroll to zoom.
  • The bar at the bottom sets the camera, the orbits, the music volume and the speed.

This site plays music. Mute it or turn it down in the bar at the bottom.

Portfolio

  • Planets have moons. Click one to see what it is.
